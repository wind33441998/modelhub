Retrieve your ModelHub license key

Enter the email address you used at Gumroad checkout. We'll look up the license key issued for your purchase.

  • 1. Copy your license key above.
  • 2. Open the ModelHub Dashboard — run modelhub ui in your terminal, or just double-click modelhub.exe (it auto-opens the dashboard).
  • 3. Paste the key and click Activate, then restart ModelHub.
